Bulk import players

Add many players at once from an Excel spreadsheet or from Zoho CRM. You'll see a preview and confirm before anything is created. Each new player gets a card and an invitation email to create their own password.

Player accounts

Each player signs in with their own email address and password. Invitation links expire after 7 days and reset links after 24 hours; each works once. Passwords are never visible to admins.

Branding

Set the logo shown at the top of the Arena and the colours used on the cards. Changes apply for everyone who opens the game.

Logo

PNG or SVG with a transparent background works best. It sits on the dark top centre of the Arena, so a white or light version reads best. Max 2 MB.

Card colours

The picture panel behind each character uses a deeper shade of the same colour, and the name text switches to dark ink on light colours so it stays readable.

Logins

The admin login is shared by the game's admins. Changing its password signs out everyone using it. Players sign in with their own email address and password; invite them and manage their accounts in the Card forge.
